Our aim 01

Conon Labs is developing an integrated space-communications stack for CubeSat and small-satellite missions, comprising:

01

S-band transceiver

A CubeSat-compatible S-band transceiver providing full control of the RF and protocol stack.

02

Dynamic access

A dynamic access protocol enabling multiple satellites to connect to a ground station opportunistically, without fixed pre-scheduled passes.

03

Ground network

A scalable ground-station network (SDR-based and custom RF hardware) with a unified mission-operations platform for commanding, telemetry, scheduling, and network management.
