# Research

## Areas of Investigation

### Radiation-Hardened Electronics
Developing strategies and component methodologies for electronics operating in high-radiation orbital environments.

### Autonomous Navigation Systems
Research into sensor fusion algorithms and edge-AI for real-time navigation without ground station dependency.

### Low-Power Telemetry Protocols
Designing energy-efficient communication stacks for CubeSat and IoT applications in bandwidth-constrained scenarios.
