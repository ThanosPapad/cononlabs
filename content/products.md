# Products

## COMMUNICATION IP & NETWORK PROTOCOL
A next-generation satellite communication protocol designed for dynamic, real-time access to ground stations.  

Instead of relying on pre-scheduled communication windows, the system enables satellites to connect opportunistically as soon as they enter visibility. The protocol manages discovery, coordination, and slot allocation across multiple satellites in real time.  

Designed to operate across both flight systems and ground infrastructure, forming the core intelligence layer of the network.

## FLIGHT SYSTEMS
S-band communication hardware and embedded systems designed for CubeSat-class missions.  

The transceiver integrates directly with our communication protocol, enabling satellites to participate in a dynamic ground station network without additional mission-specific software complexity.  
Built for:
- CubeSat and small satellite platforms
- High-reliability space environments
- Full control of RF and protocol stack behavior

## GROUND STATION HARDWARE
Scalable ground station systems designed for modern satellite communication workloads.  

Compatible with both SDR-based setups and dedicated RF architectures, enabling flexible deployment from single experimental stations to distributed networks.  
Optimized for:
- High utilization efficiency
- Multi-satellite access
- Integration with automated scheduling systems

## MISSION OPERATIONS PLATFORM
A unified software system for managing satellite communications across distributed ground stations.  

Operators can monitor satellites, manage communications, and coordinate data flow through a single interface, reducing the complexity of working with multiple ground station providers.  

Key capabilities include automated scheduling, telemetry handling, and network-wide coordination.

## GROUND STATION NETWORK
A distributed communication layer designed for scalable satellite operations.  

The system enables satellites to access available ground stations dynamically, improving communication availability and reducing idle infrastructure time compared to traditional pass-based scheduling systems.  
Built for:
- Small satellite operators
- Constellation-scale missions
- High-frequency data downlink applications

## TEST & DEVELOPMENT PLATFORM
A modular hardware and software environment for prototyping and validating satellite communication systems.  

Supports real-world emulation of orbital conditions including Doppler effects, signal variation, and multi-satellite communication scenarios, enabling rapid development and testing of new communication technologies.
