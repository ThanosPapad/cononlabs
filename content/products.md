      <h3>Communication IP &amp; Network Protocol</h3>
      <p>A next-generation satellite communication protocol designed for dynamic, real-time access to ground stations.</p>
      <p>Instead of relying on pre-scheduled communication windows, the system enables satellites to connect opportunistically as soon as they enter visibility. The protocol manages discovery, coordination, and slot allocation across multiple satellites in real time.</p>
      <p>Designed to operate across both flight systems and ground infrastructure, forming the core intelligence layer of the network.</p>
    </div>

      <h3>Flight Systems</h3>
      <p>S-band communication hardware and embedded systems designed for CubeSat-class missions.</p>
      <p>The transceiver integrates directly with our communication protocol, enabling satellites to participate in a dynamic ground station network without additional mission-specific software complexity.</p>
      <div class="re-list-label">Built for:</div>
      <ul class="re-list">
        <li>CubeSat and small satellite platforms</li>
        <li>High-reliability space environments</li>
        <li>Full control of RF and protocol stack behavior</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <h3>Ground Station Hardware</h3>
      <p>Scalable ground station systems designed for modern satellite communication workloads.</p>
      <p>Compatible with both SDR-based setups and dedicated RF architectures, enabling flexible deployment from single experimental stations to distributed networks.</p>
      <div class="re-list-label">Optimized for:</div>
      <ul class="re-list">
        <li>High utilization efficiency</li>
        <li>Multi-satellite access</li>
        <li>Integration with automated scheduling systems</li>

      <h3>Mission Operations Platform</h3>
      <p>A unified software system for managing satellite communications across distributed ground stations.</p>
      <p>Operators can monitor satellites, manage communications, and coordinate data flow through a single interface, reducing the complexity of working with multiple ground station providers.</p>
      <p>Key capabilities include automated scheduling, telemetry handling, and network-wide coordination.</p>
    </div>

      <h3>Ground Station Network</h3>
      <p>A distributed communication layer designed for scalable satellite operations.</p>
      <p>The system enables satellites to access available ground stations dynamically, improving communication availability and reducing idle infrastructure time compared to traditional pass-based scheduling systems.</p>
      <div class="re-list-label">Built for:</div>
      <ul class="re-list">
        <li>Small satellite operators</li>
        <li>Constellation-scale missions</li>
        <li>High-frequency data downlink applications</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <p>A modular hardware and software environment for prototyping and validating satellite communication systems.</p>
      <p>Supports real-world emulation of orbital conditions including Doppler effects, signal variation, and multi-satellite communication scenarios, enabling rapid development and testing of new communication technologies.</p>
