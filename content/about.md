# About

## Who We Are

Conon Labs is a deep-tech company focused on space communications and advanced electronic systems.

We develop technologies that improve how satellites communicate with Earth, combining RF engineering, embedded systems, communication protocols, and network infrastructure into integrated solutions for the NewSpace industry.

**Our mission** is to reduce the barriers to accessing space communications by creating more efficient, scalable, and cost-effective communication architectures for the next generation of satellite missions.


**The end goal** is a future where satellite communications are no longer constrained by rigid scheduling and underutilized infrastructure.

As the number of satellites in orbit continues to grow, communication systems must evolve from static resource allocation toward intelligent, dynamic networks capable of adapting in real time.

**Conon Labs** develops the technologies that enable this transition.

### Steps to achieve that

- **Develop and Validate** the Communication Hardware
Design and develop a CubeSat-compatible S-band transceiver that provides full control over the communication stack, from RF hardware to protocol implementation.  
This integrated approach enables rapid iteration, realistic testing, and validation of advanced communication concepts that cannot be achieved using off-the-shelf satellite radios.
- **Demonstrate Dynamic Communication Protocols**  
Implement and validate a dynamic access protocol capable of supporting multiple satellites within the visibility region of a single ground station.  
Using commercial SDR platforms such as the USRP B200 alongside custom transceiver hardware, the system will be tested under representative operational conditions, including Doppler effects, varying link budgets, and simultaneous communication requests. 

- **Develop a Scalable Ground Segment**  
Create a scalable ground station architecture that supports both commercially available SDR platforms for rapid development and validation, as well as custom RF hardware developed in-house for operational deployments.  
By leveraging technology derived from the same communication systems used in the space segment, the ground infrastructure maintains full control over the RF stack, enables advanced protocol implementation, and provides a cost-effective path toward a high-performance, scalable ground station network.

- **Build a Unified Mission Operations Platform**  
Develop a centralized cloud-based platform that connects all ground station assets into a single operational network.  
Satellite operators will access commanding, telemetry, scheduling, and network management services through a unified interface, eliminating the complexity of coordinating individual communication passes.  
- **Validate a Dynamic Ground Station Network**  
Demonstrate end-to-end operation of the complete system using multiple transceiver nodes, ground station infrastructure, and realistic orbital communication emulation. The objective is to validate increased ground station utilization, automated communication allocation, and scalable multi-satellite operation in a representative LEO communication environment.

## Our Values

- **Precision** – Every component, from RF design to embedded firmware, is engineered to strict technical specifications required for space-grade reliability.  

- **Reliability** – We design systems that must perform under real operational constraints, where communication failures directly impact mission success.  

- **Systems Thinking** – We develop complete end-to-end architectures, integrating space hardware, ground infrastructure, and network software into a unified communication system.  

- **Validation-Driven Engineering** – Every concept is iteratively tested through simulation, emulation, and hardware prototypes to ensure measurable performance before deployment.  

- **Innovation** – We push the boundaries of embedded systems, RF engineering, and autonomous communication protocols to enable new satellite network capabilities.  

- **Rapid Iteration** – We develop and refine technologies quickly through continuous prototyping and testing, accelerating the path from concept to validated system.
